This reference implementation demonstrates deterministic, audit-ready PDF invoice line-item extraction paired with strict arithmetic reconciliation and token-level bounding-box provenance. Across 120 synthetic invoices spanning 6 challenging layouts (standard grids, 2-line wrapped headers with multi-line descriptions, multi-page tables, negative credits and discounts, interleaved group subtotals, and ±2.5pt OCR coordinate jitter), the geometry-first parser achieves 1.0000 overall field F1, 120/120 exact grand-total checksum reconciliation, and 100.00% valid bounding-box provenance at 16.83 ms p50 and 33.11 ms p95 latency per PDF. Every currency field parses to exact integer cents to eliminate floating-point drift, and a strict-gated fallback interface only activates on checksum failures while rejecting any proposed number not grounded in actual PDF tokens.
